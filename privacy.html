WEBSITE INFORMATION

Privacy notice.

Last updated: 31 October 2026

At DESTINO, we respect your privacy and aim to handle your personal information responsibly. This notice explains what information may be collected when you use our website and how we use it.

1. Information we collect

When you submit an enquiry through our website, we may collect the following information:

  • Your name
  • Your phone number
  • Your email address, if provided
  • The service you are enquiring about
  • Your message and journey-related details, if provided

Please do not submit passwords, payment card details, medical records or other highly sensitive personal information through our general website enquiry form.

2. How we use your information

Information submitted through our website may be used to:

  • Respond to your enquiry.
  • Contact you about the service you requested.
  • Coordinate and manage journey-related requests.
  • Maintain enquiry records and improve our services.
  • Communicate relevant updates about your enquiry.

3. How your information is stored

Website enquiries are submitted to a database hosted through Supabase, which DESTINO uses to store and manage enquiry information.

Access to enquiry records is intended to be restricted to authorised DESTINO administrators for business-related purposes.

We take reasonable steps to protect information against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure or loss. However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

4. Third-party services

Our website uses third-party services to operate certain features, including:

  • Supabase: for database storage and management of customer enquiries.
  • Google Fonts: to display website typography.
  • WhatsApp: if you choose to contact us using the WhatsApp link on our website.

These services may process information according to their respective privacy policies and terms.

5. Sharing of information

DESTINO does not intend to sell your personal information. Information may be shared with service providers or other parties where necessary to respond to your request, coordinate a service, meet a legal obligation or protect legitimate business interests.

We aim to limit information sharing to what is reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose.

6. Data retention

Enquiry information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to requests, manage business operations, maintain relevant records and meet applicable legal requirements.

Retention periods depend on the purpose and nature of the information. We aim to securely delete or dispose of information when it is no longer required.

7. Your choices and requests

You may contact DESTINO to request information about the personal information you have submitted, request correction of inaccurate details or raise a concern about how your information is handled.

Requests will be handled subject to applicable law and any legitimate record-keeping requirements.
